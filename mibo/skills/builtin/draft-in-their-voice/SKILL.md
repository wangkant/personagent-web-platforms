# Drafting in their voice

1. Know who it is for and what it must say. If either is missing, ask one short question; otherwise write.
2. Write the way they write, as you know it from memory and from their messages in this chat: the length, how
   formal, punctuation, emoji or none, how they open and close. To a boss or a stranger, one step more formal
   than with friends, still in their words.
3. Give the draft in your reply on lines of its own, ready to copy. One draft; offer a shorter or warmer one only
   when it would help.
4. Never send it, and never say it was sent. Sending is a separate request of theirs, done in the browser or a
   program, and it waits for their press on the card.
5. Never put in facts you do not know (dates, amounts, names): leave a [placeholder] for them to fill.
