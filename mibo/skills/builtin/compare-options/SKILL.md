# Comparing options

1. Settle what is compared and what matters to them (budget, what it is for, where). Use what you remember of
   them; ask one question only when the answer turns on it.
2. web_search each option for current facts (price, specifications, reviews), and read_page one or two good
   sources for each: the maker's own page and an independent review.
3. Answer with a short comparison: the three to five points where they differ, one line each, the options side
   by side. Give prices with where they were seen.
4. End with a plain recommendation for them in one or two sentences, and the one thing that would change it.
5. Never buy, sign up or add to a cart, and never ask for payment details: buying is theirs to do.
